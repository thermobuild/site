Crown Square, Galway

Crown Square is a major mixed-use campus on the Monivea Road, Galway, being delivered by JJ Rhatigan & Company in partnership with the Land Development Agency, comprising 345 residential units alongside office space and a hotel. ThermoBuild Systems is currently on site installing EWI render systems across the residential blocks, with first homes expected in late 2026.
