Soffit Insulation

Soffits represent a critical area of the building envelope where cold bridging, condensation and heat loss can occur if not correctly insulated. ThermoBuild Systems provides specialist soffit insulation to all types of soffit construction, ensuring compliance with energy regulations and preventing thermal defects.

We regularly deliver soffit insulation packages on multi-storey residential and carpark developments, coordinating closely with other trades to protect programme.

  • Eliminates cold bridging at soffit junctions
  • Prevents condensation and associated dampness
  • Improves overall building energy performance
  • Applied to exposed concrete soffits, precast panels and steel frames
  • Various insulation board systems available

Typical Applications: Apartment developments, multi-storey residential, carpark soffits, commercial buildings.
