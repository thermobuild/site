Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our insulation services, quotes, materials and project process.

General

We operate nationwide, delivering insulation projects in all 32 counties across the Republic of Ireland. Our base is in Dublin, and our teams travel to sites throughout Leinster, Munster, Connacht and Ulster (Republic of Ireland counties).

We work with both. The majority of our work is delivered as a specialist subcontractor to main contractors and developers on new-build residential and commercial schemes, but we also carry out insulation upgrades directly for homeowners and property owners.

Yes. We carry full public liability insurance and comply with all Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (Construction) Regulations. Certificates of insurance are available on request for main contractors and clients.

Services & Process

We install EWI render systems, stonewool cavity wall insulation, rigid brick cavity wall insulation, soffit insulation, internal wall insulation and floor insulation. Visit our Services page for full details of each system.

Yes — the majority of our projects are delivered to main contractor programmes on active construction sites. We are experienced in coordinating with other trades and meeting agreed sequencing and deadlines.

In most cases we supply and install using certified materials from trusted manufacturers, though we're also happy to install client-supplied materials where specified by the design team.

Quotes & Pricing

Simply complete our online quote request form or call us directly on 083 448 5000. Please include as much detail as possible — location, service required, approximate size and any drawings — to help us provide an accurate estimate.

No, quotations are provided free of charge and without obligation. For larger projects we will typically arrange a site visit to confirm scope before pricing.

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 24 hours on working days, with a formal quotation typically following within a few working days depending on project complexity.

Materials & Compliance

Yes. All of our insulation systems are installed to current Part L (Conservation of Fuel and Energy) requirements and relevant Irish and European standards, and to manufacturer installation specifications.

Depending on the system and specification, we install stonewool (mineral wool), PIR, EPS and phenolic insulation boards, along with EWI render systems from leading manufacturers.

Manufacturer system warranties apply where we install to full specification using approved materials and details. We're happy to discuss specific warranty terms for your project.

Timelines & Scheduling

This varies significantly by project size and system — from a few days for a single dwelling to several months for a large apartment scheme. We'll provide a realistic programme as part of your quotation.

We do our best to accommodate urgent requirements where possible. Please call us directly on 083 448 5000 to discuss timing for time-sensitive projects.
